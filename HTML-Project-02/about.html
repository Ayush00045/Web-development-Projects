About Inception

Inception is a science fiction action thriller film directed by Christopher Nolan. The movie explores dreams, the subconscious mind, and the idea of entering another person's dreams.

About the Director

Christopher Nolan is a British-American filmmaker known for creating complex and visually ambitious films. He directed and wrote Inception.

About the Movie

  • Title: Inception
  • Release Year: 2010
  • Director: Christopher Nolan
  • Genre: Science Fiction, Action, Thriller
  • Language: English

Story Concept

The story follows Dom Cobb, a skilled thief who enters people's dreams to steal valuable information. He is given a difficult task to plant an idea inside someone's mind instead of stealing one.

Main Cast

  • Leonardo DiCaprio as Cobb
  • Joseph Gordon-Levitt as Arthur
  • Elliot Page as Ariadne
  • Tom Hardy as Eames
