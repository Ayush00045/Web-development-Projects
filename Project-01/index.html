Choclate Cake

A simple and delicious chocolate cake recipe for biginners.
Perfect for birthdays and celebration!
Prep Time:20 minutes
Cook Time:30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cups sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp cinnamon(option)
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 1 cup milk

steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C
  2. Slowly mix the flour,sugar,and cocoa powder
  3. Add eggs and milk,stir until smooth.
  4. Bake for 30 minutes or until a thoothpick comes out clean

tips

For extra flavor, add chocolate chips before baking.
You can also serve with vanilla ice cream for delicious combo!
